Privacy Policy

Last updated:

BoWave Finance Consulting ("BoWave", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what this page collects, what it deliberately does not collect, and your choices.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, phone number, company, the service you are interested in, and your message. Nothing is collected unless you fill the form and press send.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, no advertising pixels, no session recording, and no tracking cookies of any kind. We do not build a profile of your visit and we cannot see how you moved around this page.
  • The only thing this page stores in your browser is your language choice, Hebrew or English, kept locally on your own device so the page opens the way you left it. It is not sent anywhere.

How your details reach us

When you press send, your details are transmitted to a script that runs inside BoWave's own Google Workspace account, which emails them to info@bowave.finance. They do not pass through any third-party form service, CRM or marketing platform.

Third parties

  • Google provides our email and runs the script that delivers your message. This page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) is involved only if you choose to press the WhatsApp button, which opens a conversation in your own WhatsApp account.
  • This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes standard server request data in order to serve it.

How we use your details

To reply to you, to schedule a call, and to provide our services. Nothing else. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists you did not ask for.

Retention and security

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to handle them and to maintain our ordinary business records, and we apply reasonable safeguards to protect them.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing info@bowave.finance. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), additional rights may apply.
